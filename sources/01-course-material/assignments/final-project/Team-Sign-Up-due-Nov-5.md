## Final Project, Part 1: Team Sign-Up

**Worth:** 5 points, Final Project category. **Due:** Nov 5, 2026, 11:59 PM MST.

Form a team of **3 or 4 students**. Teams of 2 or 5+ are not accepted. One submission per team, and every member gets the same grade.

### Step 1: Join a group in Canvas (every member)

1. Open **People → Groups** and find the *Final Project* group set.
2. Pick one empty group (e.g. *Final Project 3*) for your team, and have **every** member join that same group. Canvas caps each group at 4.
3. Don't worry about the group's name. I rename it to your team name after the deadline.

### Step 2: One member submits this assignment

Once all of you are in the group, **one** member submits the text below for the whole team. If you submit before joining the group, the submission counts for you alone.

```
TEAM NAME: <your team name>
CANVAS GROUP: <e.g. Final Project 3>
MEMBERS (Full name, NetID; one per line):
<Full Name>, <netid>
<Full Name>, <netid>
<Full Name>, <netid>
<Full Name>, <netid>   (optional 4th)
```

**Grading:** full credit if the roster has 3–4 members, every listed member has joined the Canvas group, and it is submitted on time.

Can't find a team? Message me before the deadline and I will place you.
