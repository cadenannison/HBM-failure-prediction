### What counts

The submission is scored as a whole on a 1–5 scale. Anchor points:

* **1** — typos, formatting, broken references, a garbled sentence.
* **3** — a factual or technical error (wrong number, wrong claim about a tool or service, wrong definition) with a correct replacement.
* **5** — a substantive error or omission that changes the chapter's argument, with a corrected version and a source (documentation, paper, or your own verified test).

Scores of 2 and 4 are used for work that falls between these anchors. One sourced technical correction is worth more than ten typos. Disagreeing with a design choice is not an error; a claim that is wrong is.
