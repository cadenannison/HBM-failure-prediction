## Slide 6
- WHAT DOESN'T SHOW UP ON THE AWS BILL
- The Hidden Costs of AI: Technical Debt
- Model Debt
- Skip segment eval, calibration, MLflow tracking → restarting from scratch. Cost if skipped: ~$60K
- Data Debt
- No feature contracts, lineage, or quality gates → silent breaks. Cost if skipped: ~$45K
- Operational Debt
- No CI/CD, monitoring, canary → manual deploys, slow detection. Cost if skipped: ~$120K/yr
- Total expected debt if rigor skipped: ~$225K/yr vs. ~$33K lab investment → ~7× ROI on engineering rigor.
- CS 401R · L23 — AI Economics: Cost, ROI & FinOps
> Notes: This is the argument for a time-pressured manager who says 'just ship it, skip testing and monitoring.' Present it as a business decision: skipping rigor saves $33K but costs $228K over 24 months.
